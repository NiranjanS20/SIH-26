# Internal Team Note: Data Limitations (Do Not Expose to Users)

Please be aware of the following data limitations from the IBM Reference Data integration. First, the ferro-manganese national production series stops at 2018-19 in the official IBM statistics (`IMIG_22-23.pdf`); later years are explicitly marked "NA", so do not invent or interpolate 2020-2024 figures if this series is needed. Second, the 2024 Yearbook does not contain a plant-by-plant specification table matching the 2019 edition's Table 8; continue to cite the 2019 table for grade specifications, ensuring the 2019 source year is always visible since individual plant specs may have changed. Third, while India dropped out of the top-6 world reserve country-rankings in the 2024 edition (compared to ~4% in 2019), this reflects a change in USGS reporting/classification, not a physical shrinkage of India's reserves; do not imply a decline in reserves based solely on this comparison.
