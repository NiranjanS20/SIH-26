## 📋 Detailed Scene-by-Scene Script

### Section 1: The Hook & The Problem Statement
* **Duration:** `0:00 – 0:45` (45 seconds)
* **Screen Display:** Open [http://localhost:5173/](http://localhost:5173/) on the Landing Page. Smoothly scroll down past the Ministry of Mines & MOIL crest, highlighting the problem cards.
* **On-Screen Actions:**
  1. Hover on the hero statistics and the "What We Are Solving" cards.
  2. Highlight the three traditional bottlenecks: delayed geological reconnaissance, reactive production loss, and manual compliance compilation.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Every year, open-cast and underground manganese mining operations suffer massive revenue losses from unforeseen production shortfalls. Unpredictable monsoons, equipment downtime, and siloed borehole data force site managers to react after targets are missed.*
  > 
  > *Welcome to the **MOIL Intelligent Operations & Compliance Platform**—built for SIH 26009. We have developed a proactive intelligence layer that unifies satellite remote sensing, in-memory predictive machine learning, and automated Indian Bureau of Mines compliance into one unified system across all 10 MOIL mines."*

### Section 2: Role-Based Access Control & Portfolio Overview
* **Duration:** `0:45 – 1:30` (45 seconds)
* **Screen Display:** Click **"Login / Enter Portal"** $\rightarrow$ Show the role picker $\rightarrow$ Enter as **Admin / Corporate Executive** $\rightarrow$ Open **Portfolio View**.
* **On-Screen Actions:**
  1. Point out the 3 distinct persona roles: **HQ Executive**, **Site Manager**, and **Industry Viewer**.
  2. In the Portfolio View, display all 10 MOIL mines (Dongri Buzurg, Chikla, Kandri, Beldongri, Balaghat, Tirodi, Sitapatore, Gumgaon, Ukwa, and Munsar).
  3. Highlight the aggregate metrics: Total ROM Output vs Target, Fleet Availability, and Active Shortfall Alerts.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Enterprise security and context isolation are enforced through multi-tier Role-Based Access Control. Corporate Executives view global health across both Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh clusters, while Site Managers are strictly scoped to their assigned operations.*
  > 
  > *In this executive portfolio dashboard, leadership gets real-time visibility into overall Run-of-Mine targets, grade distribution, and early-warning alerts, allowing strategic intervention before monthly quotas slip."*

### Section 3: Core AI — Prospectivity & Production Forecasting
* **Duration:** `1:30 – 3:15` (105 seconds)
* **Screen Display:** Click on **Dongri Buzurg** (or Chikla) to transition into the **Operational Mine Workspace**.
* **On-Screen Actions:**
  1. **Tab 1: Prospectivity & Geological Model (Model 1):**
     - Switch on the interactive geospatial raster layers (NDVI, Land Surface Temperature, Clay Index, Iron Oxide, Elevation, and Soil Moisture).
     - Point out the high-confidence deposit polygons ($MnO \ge 35\%$).
     - Type a quick query into the **Geological Copilot** query bar.
  2. **Tab 2: Production Forecasting (Model 2):**
     - Navigate to the Forecast view showing the interactive Apache ECharts timeline.
     - Hover over the daily predicted curve vs the MCDR statutory target line, showing seasonal monsoon sensitivity.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Drilling down into the Dongri Buzurg operational workspace, our in-memory AI engine executes sub-millisecond inferences:*
  > 
  > *First is **Model 1: The Prospectivity Engine**. Using Sentinel multi-spectral imagery, digital elevation models, and geochemical proxies, our Random Forest pipeline classifies prospective ore zones with **91.2% accuracy** (0.94 ROC-AUC) and predicts manganese grade concentration.*
  > 
  > *Next is **Model 2: Production Forecaster**. Trained on audited historical MCDR returns and environmental volatility, this XGBoost time-series model predicts daily extraction constraints with an RMSE of just **~97 tonnes/day**, accounting for equipment availability, blasting delays, and rainfall."*

### Section 4: The Killer Feature — Root Cause (SHAP) & What-If Optimizer
* **Duration:** `3:15 – 4:45` (90 seconds)
* **Screen Display:** Open the **"Shortfall Risk"** and **"Prescriptive Actions / What-If"** tabs.
* **On-Screen Actions:**
  1. **Shortfall Risk (Model 3):** Show the dynamic categorization into **Low, Medium, or High Risk** mapped to Indian Bureau of Mines criteria.
  2. **SHAP Cause Analysis (Model 4):** Show the explainable AI waterfall chart displaying exact tonnage penalties (e.g., `-52 tonnes` due to shovel downtime, `-34 tonnes` due to blasting delays).
  3. **Prescriptive Action Optimizer (Model 5):** Drag the **Equipment Availability** slider up by 8% or reduce the **Blasting Delay** slider.
  4. Show the forecasted gap dynamically shrink to zero in real-time.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"When a mine faces a shortfall, traditional tools simply show a red number. Our platform answers: 'Why did this happen?' and 'How do we fix it today?'*
  > 
  > *Using **Model 4: SHAP TreeExplainer**, the platform attributes exact tonnage losses to specific operational bottlenecks—such as 52 tonnes lost to excavator downtime and 34 tonnes lost to blasting delays.*
  > 
  > *Then, **Model 5: The Action Optimizer** uses a 441-point grid search to back-solve the optimal adjustments. Watch as we adjust the equipment availability slider in our live What-If simulator: the model recalculates instantly, demonstrating the exact operational path to recover production and achieve targets."*

## 🎥 Video Production & Recording Checklist

- [ ] **Resolution & Aspect Ratio:** 1080p (1920x1080) at 60 FPS, 16:9 fullscreen.
- [ ] **Browser Prep:** Fullscreen browser (`F11`), zoom set to 100%, bookmarks bar hidden.
- [ ] **State Preparation:** Keep backend running on `http://127.0.0.1:8000/` and frontend on `http://localhost:5173/`.
- [ ] **Pacing Tip:** Keep cursor movements deliberate; do not shake or circle the mouse rapidly. Pause for half a second after each click before speaking the next point.
- [ ] **Audio:** Clear voiceover with background noise suppression; speak with steady, confident cadence.
